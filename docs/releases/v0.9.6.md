# Bloodborne for Apple Silicon 0.9.6

Pressing ⌘ while holding a key no longer closes the game.

What's new:

- **⌘ no longer closes or hides the game**
  ([#14](https://github.com/bloodborne-native/bloodborne-mac/issues/14)). If you held W to walk and
  touched ⌘, the game window took it as ⌘W and closed; Q and H did the same with Quit and Hide.
  The game's menu bar no longer has ⌘ shortcuts. Its items are still there, and the window's close
  button and the in-game Quit still close the game.
- **Compile shaders ahead of time runs again after a macOS update**, or when you move your games to
  another Mac. macOS throws away the compiled pipelines after an update, so they had to compile
  again during play. If the setting is on, the next start after an update prepares them again,
  with progress on screen.

## Known issues

- Blood on already killed enemies can look pale with bright edges after reloading a save.
- Plants can sometimes move unevenly, as if skipping frames.

## Install or update

Download the Apple Silicon DMG and replace Bloodborne.app. Saves and settings remain in
`~/Library/Application Support/bloodborne_mac`; the launcher backs up your saves before every
start. No game files are included: supply your own Bloodborne dump with update 1.09. To go back,
install 0.9.5 again from the releases page.
