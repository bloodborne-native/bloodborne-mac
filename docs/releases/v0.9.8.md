# Bloodborne for Apple Silicon 0.9.8

A walk key, and fixes for the mouse in the game's menus and in fullscreen.

What's new:

- **Walk slowly with the keyboard**
  ([#16](https://github.com/bloodborne-native/bloodborne-mac/issues/16)). Hold **Left Option**
  while moving and your character walks at the game's walking pace, with the keys or a
  controller's stick. You can bind it to another key in the launcher's **Controls → Movement and
  camera → Walk (hold)**. From bbport 0.7.
- **The mouse's side buttons work in the game's menus**
  ([#18](https://github.com/bloodborne-native/bloodborne-mac/issues/18)). Before, they did
  nothing while a menu was open. The middle and side buttons now do what they're bound to there
  too (the side buttons are L1 and R2 by default). Left click still confirms and right click
  still goes back.
- **The mouse no longer gets lost after a menu in fullscreen**
  ([#17](https://github.com/bloodborne-native/bloodborne-mac/issues/17)). With a menu open, the
  pointer could reach the screen's top or bottom edge, where macOS shows the menu bar or the
  Dock, and the game lost the mouse until you clicked. In fullscreen the pointer now stays just
  inside those edges.
- **Resolutions above 1080p and upscaled scenes start at their render size again.** Since 0.9.5
  the app couldn't work out the render size at start, and your log began with
  `bin/bb-probe: No such file or directory`.

Change controls in the launcher's **Controls** tab, not in `bbport.ini`: the launcher writes that
file again each time the game starts.

## Known issues

- Blood on already killed enemies can look pale with bright edges after reloading a save.
- Plants can sometimes move unevenly, as if skipping frames.

## Install or update

Download the Apple Silicon DMG and replace Bloodborne.app. Saves and settings remain in
`~/Library/Application Support/bloodborne_mac`; the launcher backs up your saves before every
start. No game files are included: supply your own Bloodborne dump with update 1.09. To go back,
install 0.9.7 again from the releases page.
