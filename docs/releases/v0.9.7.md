# Bloodborne for Apple Silicon 0.9.7

With Online mode on, you choose Play Online or Play Offline at the title screen, as on the PS4.

What's new:

- **Online mode shows the title screen's Play Online / Play Offline choice**
  ([#15](https://github.com/bloodborne-native/bloodborne-mac/issues/15)). The port skips this
  choice and starts offline, which suits offline play. With Online mode on, the choice was skipped
  as well. It is now shown when Online mode is on. With Online mode off, nothing changes. To force
  it either way, set `BB_SKIP_NETWORK_CHOICE=0` (show) or `=1` (skip) in Advanced → Extra environment
  variables.

## Known issues

- Blood on already killed enemies can look pale with bright edges after reloading a save.
- Plants can sometimes move unevenly, as if skipping frames.

## Install or update

Download the Apple Silicon DMG and replace Bloodborne.app. Saves and settings remain in
`~/Library/Application Support/bloodborne_mac`; the launcher backs up your saves before every
start. No game files are included: supply your own Bloodborne dump with update 1.09. To go back,
install 0.9.6 again from the releases page.
